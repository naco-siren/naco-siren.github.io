## Overview

Compositor for iPad ("the App") is a free, open-source image editor for compositing and photo work. Your privacy is important to us, and we want to be transparent: this App collects no data.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**

The App operates entirely on your device. Your projects, the images you import and your preferences are stored where you put them and never leave your device unless you move them yourself.

## Network Usage

The App makes no network requests of its own. It has no analytics, no tracking, no telemetry and no updater; updates come through the App Store.

If you keep projects in iCloud Drive, they are synced by Apple's iCloud Drive as any file in it is, under your iCloud account. **No data is sent to any server operated by the developer.**

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Photos and Files

- **Importing** images from Photos uses the system's photo picker, which runs outside the App; the App receives only the images you choose and never gains access to your library.
- **Saving an export to Photos** asks for permission to add to your library the first time, and the App uses it only to add that image. It never reads your library.
- **Files** opened from the Files app, iCloud Drive or another app are read and written in place through the system's file coordination, and only the files you choose.

## Data Storage

Projects are `.comp` packages: a manifest and the layers' images, stored as files on your device (On My iPad › Compositor), in iCloud Drive or wherever you save them. They are included in your device backups and can be deleted by deleting the files or uninstalling the App. The App keeps no other record of your work.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
