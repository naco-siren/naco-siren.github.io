## Need Help?

If you have questions, feedback, or run into issues with Compositor for iPad, please reach out:

**Email:** naconacona@icloud.com

**Bug Reports & Feature Requests:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/naco-siren/Compositor/issues) on the fork. Please don't report iPad issues to the original Compositor repository: the iPad app is an unofficial port, maintained here.

Compositor for iPad is open source — contributions are welcome! Or leave a comment below.

## FAQ

**Q: Which iPads does it run on?**
A: Any iPad that runs iPadOS 26, A12 and later. It is developed and tested on an iPad Air 3, so it stays usable on the oldest of them.

**Q: Where are my projects?**
A: A project is a `.comp` package. New ones go to On My iPad › Compositor in the Files app; you can also open and save them in iCloud Drive, or anywhere Files reaches. The App keeps nothing anywhere else.

**Q: Does it open my Mac projects?**
A: Yes. The iPad app reads and writes the same `.comp` format as Compositor for the Mac, with the same code, so a project made on either opens on the other. Put it in iCloud Drive and open it from Files.

**Q: Is this the official Compositor?**
A: No. Compositor is Robbie Tilton's open-source Mac app, under the MIT license. This is an unofficial iPad port built from a fork, and it is not reviewed or supported by him.

**Q: Does it support Apple Pencil?**
A: Yes, for painting, selecting and moving. Pressure sets the Brush's size from the first stroke; tap the Opacity label to let it set the opacity too, and the Size label to turn it off. The toolbar's Pencil button switches between Pressure Sensitivity, Pencil (no pressure) and Finger Touch. Apple Pencil (1st and 2nd generation) and Apple Pencil Pro report pressure; Apple Pencil (USB-C) has no pressure sensor, so choose Pencil there if strokes come out thin. A finger works too, and in Pencil or Pressure Sensitivity a resting hand doesn't draw. Tilt is not read yet.

**Q: What can it import and export?**
A: Import JPEG, PNG, HEIC and HEIF, TIFF, WebP, Photoshop PSD and PSB, camera RAW, OpenEXR and Radiance HDR. Export PNG and JPEG to Files, to Photos or to the share sheet.

**Q: Are there sample projects?**
A: Three, at [compositor-samples](https://github.com/naco-siren/compositor-samples): download a `.comp` and open it from Files.

**Q: Does it collect anything?**
A: No. Nothing leaves your device, and the App makes no network requests of its own. See our [Privacy Policy](/apps/compositor/privacy/).
